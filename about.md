I’m Sepehr Samavati, a person of many things. I got my bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Tehran and my master’s in Automation and Control Engineering from Politecnico di Milano in Italy. Since September 2026, I’ve been a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Queen’s University in Canada, focusing on robotics.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a software engineer in Italy: at HCLTech (through Experis) on Ericsson’s modems and embedded Linux, and at STIGA on the software of autonomous mowing robots. Embedded software and robotics are where I’ve built my experience, but that doesn’t stop me from diving into other fields. I consider myself strong in algorithms, optimization, and software design. And even though I’m following the engineering and research path right now, I know that leadership is where I truly belong. It has always been my passion, and it’s what people know me for.

Beyond my career, I have a deep interest in photography. I’m constantly learning and exploring my own style, with the goal of becoming a professional. I love capturing photos that tell a story through contrast or deeper meaning.

In my personal life, I enjoy hanging out with friends and meeting new people. When it comes to sports, I like running, hiking, volleyball, and ping pong. I’m also a pretty good cook, and I love playing video games in my free time.
