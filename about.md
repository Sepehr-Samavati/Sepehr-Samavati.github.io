I’m Sepehr Samavati, a person of many things. I got my bachelor’s degree in Iran from the University of Tehran and my master’s from Politecnico di Milano in Italy. I’ve been working as a software engineer for the past three years, contributing to projects at 7SangTeam, HCLTech, and Ericsson.

While my career is currently focused on embedded software development, that doesn’t stop me from diving into other fields. I consider myself strong in algorithms, optimization, and software design. And even though I’m following the engineering path right now, I know that leadership is where I truly belong. It has always been my passion, and it’s what people know me for.

Beyond my career, I have a deep interest in photography. I’m constantly learning and exploring my own style, with the goal of becoming a professional. I love capturing photos that tell a story through contrast or deeper meaning.

In my personal life, I enjoy hanging out with friends and meeting new people. When it comes to sports, I like hiking, volleyball, and ping pong. I’m also a pretty good cook, and I love playing video games in my free time.
